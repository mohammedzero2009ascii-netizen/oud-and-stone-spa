Our approach

We named the spa after two things you will notice the moment you walk in: the scent of oud in the air, and warm stone under your feet.

Unhurried

Every booking includes 20 minutes in the lounge before and after. We never stack treatments back to back.

Private

Six suites, separate ladies' and gentlemen's hammams, and a female therapist whenever you ask.

Honest

Clear prices, no add-ons pushed during your treatment, and products you can ask about, not buy.
